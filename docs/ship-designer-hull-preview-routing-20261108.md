8 November 2026. Source candidate for the owner's request to show the Art director's new models in the ship designer. Native model import, appearance and workflow acceptance remain unfinished.

The designer previously routed only exact `hull.utility.v1` to the illustrative modular Galaxy study. The utility study retains its existing mapping. Unknown identities retain the schematic fallback; names and roles do not select models or grant hull availability.

Fixed exteriors retain orbit and zoom, pending-preview versus draft-fit labels, existing availability checks and owned-stage cleanup. Nacelle, deflector and internal-core study controls are hidden because those exteriors have no received component/socket map. Installed equipment remains authoritative in the designer's facts. Fixed-hull captions retain their actual hull label instead of being overwritten by the modular Galaxy caption. Credits describe the currently installed resources: the existing Defiant is the owner-provided Meshy study, not the newly approved A. Klemm model.

This change installs no new model, material, importer, socket, gameplay rule or save schema. The approved Defiant and Galaxy GLBs still require their separate receiving work: exact provenance and resource identities, material assignments, UVs, alpha/glass, emission, dependency closure, framing and disposal. Their approved replacement credits must change with the verified replacement resources. The old one-slot/opaque material adapter is not acceptance of those new GLBs.
