# Integrated activities campaign — provisional playtest

## Status and identities

The portable source candidate implements an explicit new activity campaign. Unity
integration and actual player acceptance remain pending. This is reversible
playtest content, not final balance. Earlier V1/V2 generated campaigns and Format8
published-construction campaigns retain their original rules, bytes, loaders and
unsupported-source decisions; there is no conversion or fallback.

The new root is Format9, `generated-campaign-activities-v1`, policy
`generated-activities-mixed-production-v1`, with an independently checked nested
`generated-campaign-owned-core-activities-v1` marker. A trusted external package
pins the complete catalogue, ready classes, v3 tactical/activity support, content,
placement rule, actual replay limit and retained-record budget. A saved hash is a
consistency check, never permission to supply support metadata.

## Actual first content

Three real ready classes compile through the ordinary ship designer and the full
v3 support compiler: Constitution Explorer (study3/contact1/one beam), Galaxy
Cruiser (study4/contact3/two beams), and Defiant Combat (study1/contact0/three
beams). Their exact costs, capacities, mounts, crew and power are authored in
`scenarios/activities-v1/`; no art asset supplies statistics. All three can be
published normally. Unsupported custom fittings remain publishable but cannot
enter this production stream. Survey capabilities are powered compiled outputs;
study and contact require a stationary owned participant at the observed site.

Every actor's paid ship and structure orders share one FIFO per colony. One head
receives actual Industry each turn, with at most one completion and no carry.
Credits and local strategic resources are paid once up front. Cancellation uses
the existing proportional unused-work refund. Frozen paid R1 remains R1 after R2
publication or later access changes. Core production queues are empty at every
published boundary; a transient detached projection reuses the shared paid ship
handler, production, pool/provenance and refund rules. No transient queue escapes.

Fabrication annexes, research laboratories and hydroponic extensions add actual
infrastructure only after completion. Economy precedes Production, so new output
starts in the following eligible cycle. Existing economy semantics apply: power
shortage scales Industry, not Research or Food. Installed/pending uniqueness is
one of each new structure per colony.

Four finite sites provide pulsar, spectrum, archive and derelict investigations.
Science rewards use only the new grant's budget in the named discipline; old
banks and allocation weights are preserved. Surplus beyond the maximum science
level is explicitly unlearnable, not fungible science. Previews expose the exact
learnable/unlearnable split. Progression receipts are retained on the typed finding,
leaving the older core receipt schema unchanged. Derelict risk is disclosed and
bounded; this first risk policy cannot itself reduce a surviving ship below one
hull. It transfers no physical salvage or cargo.

The Ilex Survey Commune and Namar Local Council are persisted independent minor
governments with distinct body and species identities. An actor must observe the
site before knowing its facts, request contact, and obtain consent before the
cooperation or credits-funded aid branch is offered. Refusal grants no access or
political control. Aid transfers funding only, never cargo or world ownership.

The placement rule `activity-placement-frontier-v2` gives each home a nearby
science opportunity and minor encounter, chosen deterministically near three
ordinary crossings. Two further sites are wider discoveries. Placement does not
chart them, reveal markers or provide private routes.

## Finite work, privacy and transactions

Finding identity is actor/site/stable finding ID. Baseline and deep alternatives
share one budget and cannot both be claimed. Accepted funding is consumed even
if the participant leaves, reorganizes, is destroyed, aborts or misses its finite
deadline. Work lasts at most three actual advances; an earned but unclaimed
finding retains one final claim/abort journal liability. Claim rechecks the exact
participant, location, positive hull, powered capability and current relation.

Actor observations contain only observed sites, current relation-permitted choice
captions/prerequisites/costs/known risk, own ships, colonies, revisions, queues and
findings. Future outcome scripts and consent-gated branches are omitted. Reports
appear only on committed own findings. Mutable DTO observations are deep copies
and never authorization tokens.

All player commands and automatic AI procurement validate detached candidates.
Payment, source ledgers, mixed queues, structures, findings, relations,
observations, caches, names, transcripts and IDs publish together after full
validation and canonical physical-core synchronization. The outer ordered
journal replays both common and activity operations against the saved frozen map,
then compares the whole resulting root. Load never regenerates the map.

The new journal reserve enumerates all remaining positive-addition structure
combinations, actual policies, resistance states and disruption regimes through
the actual economy projection. It takes minimum Industry and maximum Food,
reserves all-owner mixed FIFO work, possible ship encounters, current combat debt
and final decisions. Its aggregate technical projection-work bound is80million
conservative primitive-work units, accommodating up to eight structure outcomes;
this does not change the older fixed-infrastructure calculator's10million bound.
The queued-world food check is a direct queued-world headroom check, not a global
economy safety proof. Ordinary unrelated technical errors still reject atomically.
Budgets are technical supported domains, not adopted gameplay fleet limits.

## Receiving scope

Source/CPU evidence must prove legal ready fits, actual paid mixed completion,
frozen revisions, actor-known discovery, targeted grants, consent transitions,
participant lifecycle, late-fault rollback, exact replay, privacy and unchanged
legacy golden behavior. Actual Unity import, layout, picking, artwork, rendering,
background persistence, player build and native journeys require separate receiving
evidence. Source compilation alone makes none of those claims.
