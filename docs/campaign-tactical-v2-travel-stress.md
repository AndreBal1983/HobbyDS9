## Focused boundary audit

- **Input ownership:** mutating the source's fleets, graph and contacts after Start, and mutating each submitted travel payload after acceptance, does not alter the owned session.
- **Frozen proof ownership:** clearing the exported pre-movement fleets and receipt collection does not alter live state. Corrupting the last request's ship hull rejects reload after earlier valid requests have been inspected, with no mutation of the original session.
- **Component merging:** an arrival can connect two pending components via its own follow target and an existing participant's follow intent. The test retains all five memberships and individual stop times, including members of the removed component. Canonical request copying/replay and reversed input ordering agree.
- **Batch atomicity:** an injected exception at the last aftermath entry, after the prior eleven entries staged their mutations, leaves the completed round histories and all actual pools/strategic state byte-identical. Clearing the probe allows the normal finalization to succeed. The probe is the existing internal test seam, not a production API.
- **Defense isolation:** exact two-fleet request rosters prevent unrelated neighboring traffic or separate encounters entering a tactical snapshot. Independent traffic stocks and reversed encounter resolution agree. The inherited B1 fleet-local defensive-package regression remains required; this report does not assert a new defensive or allegiance mechanic.
