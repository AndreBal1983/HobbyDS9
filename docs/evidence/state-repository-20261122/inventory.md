## Safe-boundary interpretation

Proposed capture occurs only after successful **outer adoption**. Planning after completed turn/finalization, complete AwaitingCombat, accepted buffered results and valid tactical commitment/round checkpoints can be candidates if their edition exporter accepts the full root. Inner phase callbacks, preview/drawing and failed transactions are not triggers. Preserve complete requests/results so load never reruns completed strategic phases, reseeds combat or refills physical pools. Current journals and private roots are not actor-safe multiplayer replication.
