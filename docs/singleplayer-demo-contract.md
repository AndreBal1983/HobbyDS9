## Early binding contract

`CampaignSingleplayerDemoApplication.StartPackage(CampaignSingleplayerDemoPackageDto)` / `LoadSave(package, CampaignSingleplayerDemoSaveDto)` return `ICampaignSingleplayerDemoSession : ICampaignIntegratedPlaytestSession`. Additive methods are `CaptureDemo()` (immutable `HumanActorId`, `ObjectiveNodeId`, `LastTurn`, `Turn`, `Phase`, `Outcome`, `ObjectiveText` only) and `ExportDemoSave()` (trusted persistence host only). Existing strategy, own ship, contact, travel, round and aftermath APIs remain in use for **HumanActorId only**. No enemy-actor switching is needed or permitted. The separate actual demo facade implements this capability; old integrated factories do not. Never capability-cast a legacy host and assume it is a demo.

`AdvanceTurn(turn, revision)` stages AI economy/production/research and fleet orders inside the whole-turn transaction. It never advances automatically merely because a screen renders. Enemy round choices commit deterministically from lawful round-start observations before human orders, with the same fleet/target validation. Human `CommitRound` remains explicit and resolves only one joint round. `FinalizeBatch` stays a separate explicit human action so reports are not dismissed automatically. No automatic human orders, hidden counter-picking, extra resource bonuses or ammunition refill.

The complete new save is wrapper **4**, profile `campaign-tactical-v2-singleplayer-demo-1`, containing integrated3/physical2/lifecycle1/campaign14/content11. Bare legacy/physical/integrated saves are rejected by this loader; no migration. Package binds `HumanActorId`, `OpponentActorId`, `ObjectiveNodeId`, `LastTurn`, `AiPolicyId`, `ObjectivePolicyId` and the exact integrated package. Persisted opponent plan contains policy/actor/goal/last-planned turn; accepted commands, fleets, stocks, queues and round commitments remain in the same nested authoritative root. The fixed public defensive goal has no RNG, hidden forecast or unbounded search state. Last-planned boundary is checked against the real saved phase/turn. Exporting inherited nested roots fails closed at both facade and direct backend. Rules `science-engineering-v1` and compiler `ship-design-compiler-v1` remain unchanged.

## Provisional bounded scenario

One human Federation and a Cardassian AI contest **Bajor**. The AI has a persistent, public scenario goal to defend Bajor, buys affordable admitted cruisers and routes its own fleets there through known topology. It may use only owned resources/ships, public prices and lawful contacts; no future player route or private orders. Other authored civilizations are inert AI with no combat role in this bounded scenario.

Objective policy `bajor-surviving-hull-presence-demo-v1`: at the end of resolving turn12, sum surviving current hull of stationary human and opponent ships at Bajor. Greater human hull wins; less or no human presence loses; equal nonzero hull is a stalemate. Destroying the opponent's military earlier wins; losing every human ship loses. Only the trusted terminal evaluator reads both actual forces; it returns no hidden enemy count/position/stocks or midgame strength ratio to the renderer/planner. Evaluation waits for pending combat aftermath; it never bypasses a battle to declare success. This is a bounded military-presence objective, not invasion, ownership transfer or diplomacy. The earlier absence-of-all-enemies proposal produced a draw despite useful fighting, so this explicitly versioned reversible demo criterion replaces it before integration. Contact freshness remains finite20 turns, longer than the bounded demo, and current encounters produce lawful renewed observations. No infinite tracking is introduced.

Demo content gives each participating side 300 starting credits; shared admitted cruiser costs120 credits +5 Dilithium +16 Industry; both use the same institute Research50 and existing fast pace5000. These are explicit new scenario values, not AI-only gifts/validator bypasses or permanent global balance. Existing fits and finite shield/ammunition stocks remain exact. Real first completion is resolvingturn2. The AI's procurement goal is three ships, not a fleet-size rule; it spends only affordable owned stocks and may fail local yard/resource validation normally. It routes to the public objective, holds/engages there, keeps locked partial-edge travel, and never follows hidden future paths. Tactical policy receives only own/visible condition snapshots: stable visible weakest-hull fleet focus, Charge while healthy, Screen when no visible targets or all own members have at most one-third hull. One order per fleet; no extra opportunities. General v1-blueprint-to-v2 construction/refits remain unsupported.
