## Feature appearance and selection

- Stars remain pale, compact screen-sized points at their **persisted stellar positions**. Never move them to a region centroid. System selection and star-influence details are separate inspector actions.
- Black holes use a dark core and broken lavender ring. Their actual generation influence ellipse can be shown through Influence areas. It is not an event horizon, damage zone or sensor range.
- Nebulae use faint teal fills following only their persisted axis-aligned influence ellipses, with a small diamond centre target. Do not draw an invented cloud contour or add decorative stars. Describe the ellipse as a generation influence area.
- Region boundaries remain neutral, one pixel, opacity 0.32; selected region uses an ice outline. Feature presence does not imply ownership. An empty navigation region may contain an environmental influence without a star system.
- Star centres take hit priority over candidate brackets and feature markers. The inspector provides candidate and contributing-feature buttons so overlap never makes those details inaccessible. Nebula footprint fills do not block region picking.

All map coordinates use the same **content-box origin**, including markers, boundary painter, route endpoints and pointer conversion. Panel borders must not shift only one layer. Use uniform fitting with honest letterboxing. Scale real footprint radii with the map; keep text, markers, hit areas and outlines screen-sized.

## Explanation and controls

Features and Influence areas are presentation toggles, not switches that change generator inputs. Density causes shows persisted region influence provenance: base density, actual contributing feature names/values, capped target density and explicitly provisional tuning. Do not recompute purported authoritative contributions in the renderer. Ordinary crossing costs remain separate from these values.

The map is a full-output generator review, not actor knowledge or live sensors. Do not infer resources, gravity damage, interference, colonies, factions in space or future travel consequences. Fields not supplied by the portable contract stay absent.
