## Receiving specification for Unity

1. **Composition:** use the existing campaign shell and legal disclosed-body source. Proposed standard split: viewer/body list roughly two thirds, bounded selected-body dock roughly one third; cap dock text measure. At1280×720, preserve a useful selected portrait and action strip, scroll details locally, and collapse secondary controls/sections before shrinking text. At3440×1440 cap console/text widths and enlarge meaningful viewing space, not blank margins. Recovered design tokens:18px body/16px secondary,44px normal targets,3px focus ring; native font/DPI must be checked.
2. **Selected world:** source evidence shows `ReviewSolOverview.Draw` assigns the full stage texture plus `Image.sourceRect` from `ReviewSolStage.TryGetPortraitRect`; the latter uses camera viewport Y directly. A coordinate-origin/crop mismatch is a plausible cause of the observed wrong region, **not yet a proven engine diagnosis**. Verify an asymmetric selected-body fixture and actual UI sourceRect coordinates. Prefer a dedicated selected-body render pass/target that excludes orbit meshes, independently fitted to the globe and rings. Reuse geometry/materials, never overview screen coordinates as planet identity.
3. **Materials:** albedo sRGB exactly once, cloud/parameter masks linear, mips and proper texture filtering. Use existing Solar System Scope Earth/Mars maps with retained CC BY4.0 credit; other terrain remains labelled decorative unless an authoritative reference exists. Warm star key, cool low fill, readable day/night boundary; optional water response only where a valid decorative water mask exists. The current surface shader's on-surface rim is not an outer atmospheric silhouette: if needed, use a bounded shell/Fresnel pass with subtle day-side scattering for known atmospheres only. Proposed shell1.02–1.035 radii; no thick neon halo or atmosphere on airless/unknown worlds. Unknown surfaces stay unresolved, not terrestrial inference from name.
4. **Star/orbits:** one bounded star/corona treatment; orbit strokes in a controlled overlay or antialiased mesh with selected/normal state independent from focus. No generated extra bodies to make the system look busy. Disclosed Earth/Mars-only views stay two-body views; a full Sol showcase is a separate labelled fixture if other bodies are not disclosed.
5. **Interaction:** selection, focus, opening a world and camera movement retain their existing distinct boundaries. No simulation update from camera/animation, no apparent physical travel or survey completion. Reduced motion means stable framing; no forced automatic spin. Markers remain aligned and reachable with keyboard while labels avoid overlap.
6. **Resources/performance proposal:** one dirty-only overview pass plus one selected portrait, at most two owned targets; proposed caps1536×640 overview and512² portrait, ARGB32 plus depth24. Assuming4bytes color +4bytes depth, logical targets total9.5MiB before driver/engine copies. At720p use smaller targets matched to display footprint. Shared imported textures/meshes remain Unity-owned; cache procedural surfaces by disclosed profile, no new allocations every frame. Atmosphere adds at most one shell per visible atmospheric body; no per-star GameObjects or full-screen raymarching. Transient focus motion may redraw briefly; settled/reduced-motion view submits no idle work. Dispose on detach/host replacement/failure. Target responsive60Hz interaction; actual GPU/frame timing and memory must be measured on the receiving hardware before acceptance, not inferred from triangle/byte counts.
