## Implemented scope

Separate deterministic stage streams seed features before topology. Star/system and black-hole anchors retain their original positions as mandatory sites. Additional empty-region sites follow a bounded, capped integer influence field; exact Voronoi clipping and shared-boundary adjacency derive the graph. Ordinary edge costs remain 1. Counts, influence weights, ellipses and spacing are **provisional review tuning**, not approved balance or physical laws.

New `EnvironmentGalaxyApplication.Generate/Open` produces a detached review with export and graph-route APIs. Format2 / `generated-galaxy-review-v1` / `environment-first-regions-v3` / `provisional-environment-influence-v1` persists settings, features, tuning, full layout and sampled influence contributions. Open validates complete canonical content against exact regeneration. Old format1 v1/v2 Open and original exported maps remain unchanged, without migration.

Generation switches support controlled comparisons. Frontend visibility controls must remain separate and must not regenerate authority. Influence ellipses describe generation pressure; they do not represent sensors, damage, territory or unique attribution of an entire polygon.
