## Lawful map and next action

The player selects an own fleet and known destination, previews through actual `Current.FindKnownRoute`, reviews the named route/cost, then submits through existing `SendPreviewedTravel` and shared validation. Displayed offers require current actor/stamps and known node/lane membership. Preview, refresh, stationary presence and partial movement cannot chart branches. Weighted progress uses backend receipt values; no Unity ETA, movement budget, route cost or discovery calculation is added.

Actual arrival adds the backend's next-Planning discovery/receipt. Newly lawful branches become selectable through the same route workflow. Circular placement is explicitly schematic and changes as knowledge grows; it does not claim physical distances. Selected fleet/destination survive ordinary navigation. An advance/discovery invalidates a stale stamped preview rather than silently sending it. Receipts list only the player's admitted identities; redisplay does not append or duplicate discoveries.
