## Worked outcomes and proposed next steps

1. **Timing.** The actual faster physical source spends cost 3 with pursuer budget 6 and target budget 4: arrivals at 1/2 and 3/4, contact at Bajor at **3/4**, both stop. Shared-start departure regression lets an outgoing fleet leave despite a stationary engager; #26 later merges retain stops at 0, 1/3 and 1/2 in one frozen battle. **PROPOSED:** retain this bundle for the controlled slice and explicitly approve its departure and late-roster implications. Alternative: contact-first start can trap departing fleets; sealing the roster at first contact would instead require an explicit holding/reinforcement policy for later arrivals.
2. **Mobility.** Personal floor((speed+agility)/2) gives cruiser 65 and surveyor 47. Their lower-median fleet score is 47×(4+3)=329 versus lone cruiser 455, with tie margin 10. At equality 65, the cruiser can screen but cannot Flank an enemy median 65. Surveyor command 65 does not raise the score; per-defender sensors instead contribute 20 basis points/point to interception. **PROPOSED:** review this edge case in the first playable slice before adoption. Alternative: bounded coordination or smoother thresholds needs separate tuning/vectors; no numerical bonus is agreed.
3. **Cloak power.** Current usable 80 minus essentials 26, ordinary equipment 36 and cloak 6 leaves **12** unused while cloaked; reveal releases 6, leaving 18 unused without extra attack/recharge. **PROPOSED:** retain strict concurrent affordability for the authored slice. Alternative: explicitly chosen pre-battle power reductions can admit lower-output designs, but need allocation commands/validation and must preserve ordinary costs and agreed reveal behavior.
4. **Condition precision.** The visible current-profile enemy cruiser initially shows **220/220** hull and **40/25/25/20** shields; its seven-round launcher stock is withheld. **PROPOSED:** retain this exact condition only as an explicitly approved controlled-slice precision policy. Alternative: bands or rounded condition reduce information; target ranking and baseline AI would need the same observed precision rather than hidden exact values.
5. **Mixed hostility.** In the tested A–B encounter, a third owner hostile to B but neutral to A arrives at **1/2**. The candidate returns `InvalidBatch`; earlier contact/economy/movement are not partially committed. **PROPOSED:** keep the gate for review and design owner/side separation before allowing those encounters in a general campaign. Alternative: explicitly constrain the first authored playable scope to supported owner relationships; this avoids an allegiance implementation now but is not general diplomacy support.
6. **Provisioning.** Asking the review facade to enqueue static or blueprint construction returns `UnsupportedSource`; matching an existing cruiser design is insufficient to invent its fit or seven-round stock. **PROPOSED:** fixed inventory for controlled review, then an explicit immutable construction/provision template and initialize-once transaction for broader play. Alternative: keep new ships on an explicitly separate legacy path with reviewed encounter routing, at the cost of a mixed-system UX; no implicit conversion is acceptable.
